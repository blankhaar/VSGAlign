# Accepted manuscript figures

The authoritative figure copies come from the final A&A revision-3 source
submission dated 2026-08-04. The older arXiv v1 source is not the figure
authority.

Two figures in that submission are generated by VSGAlign:

- `single_grain_combined_a5.0.pdf`
- `size_dist_combined.pdf`

Their accepted SHA-256 checksums are recorded in
`reference_figures.sha256`. The checked-in copies under `paper/figures/` match
the final submission byte for byte.

The grain-angle schematic and the ground-state-alignment PNG are manuscript
artifacts outside VSGAlign's maintained figure workflow. The cross-section
scripts in this repository generate diagnostics that are not included in the
final revision-3 manuscript.

PDF byte identity is not expected after regeneration because plotting and PDF
libraries may write environment-dependent metadata. Release validation
therefore compares rendered content with the accepted references as well as
checking the numerical workflow for warnings and finite outputs.
